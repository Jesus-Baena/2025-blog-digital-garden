Led **extensive emergency and early recovery** WASH operations in Port-au-Prince following the 2010 Earthquake, **serving 135,000 Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs)**. My role was central to the cholera response and hurricane preparedness, involving multiple rapid assessments and the establishment of life-saving services.

- Directed large-scale WASH **operations in IDP camps**, providing safe water and sanitation to 135,000 people post-earthquake.
- Led the exploratory mission and subsequent response to the **cholera outbreak in Artibonite**, establishing WASH services in critical health centers.
- Coordinated and led **rapid assessment and response teams for the Hurricane Tomas** emergency.
