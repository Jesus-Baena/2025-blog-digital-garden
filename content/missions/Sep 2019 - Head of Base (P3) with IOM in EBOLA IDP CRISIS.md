Orchestrated a **comprehensive emergency response to the Ebola crisis** and mass internal displacement as Head of Base. I led the development and execution of critical Shelter and WASH programming, secured vital resources, and managed significant operational risks in a volatile environment to serve displaced and vulnerable communities.

- Directed **emergency response operations for the Ebola** crisis and internal displacement, managing all programmatic, financial, and logistical functions of the field base.
- **Led the complete program cycle** for Shelter and WASH interventions, from needs assessment and design to implementation and evaluation.
- **Successfully secured additional program funding** and managed base-level resources, risk, and stakeholder coordination in a highly insecure context.
