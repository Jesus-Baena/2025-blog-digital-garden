Not everything is going to be work. 

We also need some way to sort out all the bad blood we accumulate in this business day over day. To help on that, [three geniuses invented in 2015](https://jadedaid.com/our-story/index.html) a version of *cards against humanity* specifically targeting the cynical soul of the field. It was called **JadedAid.**

The [print-at-home](https://jadedaid.com/download-jadedaid-pdf-print-at-home-cards/index.html) version was made in their web page, and the rest is trashy-boozy-humanitarian history. 

![[Pasted image 20260320090531.png]]

Brilliantly, they left some blank cards for customization, opportunity that I have taken to create some cards with a local emergency touch.
